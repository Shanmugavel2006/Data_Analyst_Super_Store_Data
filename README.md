# Data_Analyst_Super_Store_Data

## Business Insights

- The business generated approximately **$2.30 million in total sales** and **$286.40K in total profit**, resulting in an overall **profit margin of 12.47%**.
- Sales showed an **overall upward trend from 2014 to 2017**, indicating continued growth over the analyzed period.
- The **West and East regions** contributed strongly to overall sales, while regional profitability varied across the business.
- **Technology** generated strong sales and profitability compared with the other product categories.
- Profitability differed significantly across sub-categories. Some sub-categories generated strong positive profits, while others showed **negative profitability** and may require further investigation.
- The **Consumer segment** contributed a significant share of total sales, followed by the Corporate and Home Office segments.
- A relatively small group of products contributed a large portion of sales, highlighting the importance of identifying and monitoring top-performing products.
- Customer-level analysis shows that a limited number of customers contribute significantly to total sales, providing opportunities for focused customer engagement.
- The dashboard also identifies **low-profit and loss-making products**, which can help the business review pricing, discounts, and product-level performance.
- Overall, the analysis provides a clear view of **sales growth, profitability, regional performance, product performance, and customer contribution**.
